The Client Transformation Blueprint

Our phased approach guarantees immediate momentum, structured scalability, and clear ROI.

1

Stage 1: The AI Audit (Diagnosis) Week 1 - 2

We interview your leadership team to understand high-level strategic goals, and on-the-ground employees to record actual process friction. We identify "Time Syncs" (repetitive admin tasks) and "Quality Risks" (manual calculations prone to error) to highlight gaps.

2

Stage 2: The Prescription (Strategy) Week 2 - 3

We map your workflows onto an Opportunity Canvas and plot them on the Opportunity Matrix (Impact vs. Effort). We collaborate with you to co-create a clear roadmap, dividing solutions into Quick Wins, Big Swings, and Nice-to-Haves.

3

Stage 3: Partner-Led Implementation Phased

We bring in our trusted developer partner network to build custom pipelines (using n8n, custom LLMs, Vapi, or Pinecone databases). By managing the roadmap but outsourcing execution, we keep overhead low and guarantee top-tier technical delivery.

4

Stage 4: Managed AI Maintenance Monthly SLA

AI models, prompt schemas, and APIs change weekly. We act as your AI MSP (Managed Service Provider) to monitor endpoints, update system instructions, and ensure your custom automations run flawlessly 24/7/365.
